vidlove

Movie and TV embed player for your website

Embed a film or a TV episode with one line of HTML. Give the iframe a TMDB id and it plays. There is no SDK, no API key and no build step.

Embed a movie

Point an iframe at /embed/movie/ and the film’s TMDB id.

<iframe src="https://player.vidlove.cc/embed/movie/155" allowfullscreen></iframe>

Embed a TV episode

Episodes add the season and the episode number.

<iframe src="https://player.vidlove.cc/embed/tv/1396/1/1" allowfullscreen></iframe>

What your viewers get

  • It plays the moment they press play, and opens at the same scene next time.
  • Subtitles they can restyle: font, size, colour and background.
  • Chromecast to the television, picture-in-picture on the laptop.
  • If one server cannot play a title the next one does, and your link never changes.
  • Controls built for thumbs as well as for a keyboard.

What you can change

Colours, control icons, your own logo, autoplay, the starting quality, subtitle styling and the player skin are all options on the same URL. Nothing is configured anywhere else.

Questions

What do I need to embed a title?
Its TMDB id. That is all.

Which ids does it take?
TMDB ids, for both films and TV.

Does it work on phones?
Yes, and it goes full screen there too.
